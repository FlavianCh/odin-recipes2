Spaghetti Aglio e Olio

Description

Spaghetti aglio e olio recipe featuring finely grated Parmigiano-Reggiano. Savory and Flavory

Ingredients

  • 1 pound uncooked spaghetti
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • 6 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes, or to taste
  • salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh Italian parsley
  • 1 cup finely grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese

Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to boil. Add spaghetti, stirring occasionally, until cooked through but firm to the bite, 10 to 12 minutes. Drain and transfer to a large pasta bowl
  2. Meanwhile, combine olive oil and garlic in a small, cold skillet
  3. Cook garlic over medium heat until tender but not brown, about 10 minutes. Reduce heat to medium-low. Continue cooking, stirring frequently, until garlic is golden brown, about 5 minutes more. Remove from heat.
  4. Toss pasta with red pepper flakes, salt, and black pepper
  5. Pour olive oil-garlic mixture over pasta. Sprinkle with Italian parsley and half the Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese toss to combine.
  6. Serve pasta topped with the remaining Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese
